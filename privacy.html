Privacy Policy

Joyan Google Drive Sync

This is a personal application used to synchronize files between authorized Google Drive accounts and a local computer.

Information accessed

The application accesses Google Drive files and metadata required to perform file synchronization. Access is granted by the Google account owner through the Google OAuth authorization process.

How information is used

Google Drive information is used solely to perform the requested synchronization between Google Drive and the user's local computer.

Data sharing

Google Drive data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The application does not use Google Drive data for advertising.

Data storage

Synchronization data is stored locally on the user's computer. OAuth credentials are stored locally by the rclone application and are not intentionally transmitted to third parties.
